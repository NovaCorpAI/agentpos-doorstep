# Terms of service, AgentPOS Doorstep

Effective 2026-09-15. Provider: NovaCorpAI SpA, Chile.

## The service

Doorstep issues signed attestations that a Ring package or doorbell event did or did not
occur inside an order's expected delivery window, based on event metadata Ring shares with
the household's authorization. Households use it free of charge. Stores pay a flat add-on.
Doorstep never holds funds, never processes payments and takes no commission.

## Households

You must own or control the Ring account you link and be entitled to authorize access to
its event metadata. You choose which stores and orders receive attestations and may revoke
at any time. You are responsible for the accuracy of the orders you associate.

## Stores

You receive attestations only for households that authorized you. You may use them as
evidence in refunds, disputes and chargebacks. You must delete an attestation when the
household requests deletion through Doorstep, and you may not resell or combine
attestations to profile households.

## Nature of the evidence

An attestation is independent evidence that an event of a given type occurred on a given
device at a given time, or that none occurred in a window. It is not a legal finding, not a
guarantee that a specific item was delivered, and not a promise about the outcome of any
refund, dispute or chargeback. Acceptance depends on the issuer, the network and the
parties involved.

## Acceptable use

No use for surveillance, tracking or identification of individuals; no cross-household
aggregation; no attempt to obtain video or media through Doorstep; no circumvention of
consent controls. Violation ends access immediately.
